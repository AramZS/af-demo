## Funding

### DONATE:

ALFALFA is fiscally sponsored by the Utah Film Center. Tax deductible donations can be made via their online portal here. Donations cannot be recouped as an investment nor do they participate in the net proceeds of the project alongside the investors, as these are considered charitable contributions. 


### INVEST:

Alfalfa is a feature-length independent motion picture being produced by A Really Good Home Pictures, Buﬀalo 8, and Glass Eye Pix through ALFALFA VENTURES LLC., a New York State limited liability company. The film is currently in the financing stage with a targeted production budget of $2,750,000. The picture is eligible for domestic and international distribution, including potential submission for festival consideration and digital platform licensing. Investors will be entitled to a share of the net profits, which shall be based on each investor’s equity capital contribution over the total equity raised toward the Budget of the project.

Please email alfalfafilm@gmail.com with any investment inquiries or to receive a complete financial summary of the project.
