ALFALFA has an accomplished team of producers with credits at top festivals (Cannes, SXSW, Sundance) – distributed by both boutique companies and major streaming entities. They have managed a wide array of productions, from microbudgets at 20K to $10M films - forming a dynamic and resourceful team that can apply the tactics of small independent filmmaking to a project with the scope and ambition of ALFALFA. The team includes Andy Fraser (Unstoppable, The Good Shepard), Stephen Mastrocola (Breakup Season, Crossword), Lu Robinson (Crumb Catcher, 2nd Chance), Joshua Elias Palmer (Transcendent, Old Soul), Nevin Kelly Fair (May December, Arco) and Rev Lennox Yearwood (Upcoming Untitled Tom McCarthy Film). 

The rest of the Alfalfa team is composed of established professionals, including the Utah-based Line Producer Scott E. Chester (Hereditary, Wild Horse Nine), Cinematographer Mateus Bastos (Gazer, Anima) and legal counsel provided by George Rush (Sorry to Bother You, Obsession).
